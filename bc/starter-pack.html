AGM / SGM notice

~20 days before

Notice must go out at least 20 days before the meeting when sent by mail, email, fax, or left at the door — 16 days if handed directly to the person. That figure is 14 days of statutory notice, plus 2 for the "at least" counting rule, plus 4 for deemed receipt where notice isn't hand-delivered.

Strata Property Act ss. 45(1), 61(3) · Interpretation Act s. 25

Budget approval

at the AGM

The strata's budget for the coming fiscal year is approved by a resolution passed by majority vote at the annual general meeting. If it's voted down, a replacement budget goes to a special general meeting, also decided by majority vote.

Strata Property Act s. 103

Records-request response

1–2 weeks

A request for records or documents must be answered within 2 weeks. A request limited to the bylaws or rules, or a request for an Information Certificate (Form B), must be answered within 1 week. The clock runs from the date the request is received.

Strata Property Act ss. 36(3), 59(1)

Depreciation report & electrical planning report

dates set by regulation

The Act requires a strata corporation to obtain both a depreciation report and an electrical planning report from a qualified person, "on or before the dates determined in accordance with the regulations." The Act itself sets no fixed interval — the schedule lives in the Strata Property Regulation, which has changed before.

Strata Property Act ss. 94, 94.1

Special levy & CRF spending — the vote to use

before you spend

A special levy split by unit entitlement needs a 3/4 vote; splitting it any other way needs a unanimous vote. Contingency reserve fund spending needs only a majority vote for a short specific list of purposes; everything else needs a 3/4 vote.

Strata Property Act ss. 108(2)(a)–(b), 96(b)(i)(A)–(B)

Why these carry citations

A compliance answer without its source is just an opinion. Every figure in these tools was read from the official consolidation at bclaws.gov.bc.ca rather than from memory or a summary site, and each page shows the section and the date it was checked. Where the law is genuinely unsettled — the 20-versus-21-day notice count, for instance — the tool says so instead of picking a side quietly.

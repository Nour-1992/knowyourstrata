BC strata compliance tools

Five free calculators for the deadlines and thresholds BC councils get wrong most often. Every answer carries the section it came from and the date that section was last checked against the official consolidation.

AGM / SGM notice calculator SPA ss. 45, 61 Pick your meeting date, get the last legal day to send notice — including the counting rules that turn "2 weeks" into about 20 days. Special levy calculator SPA ss. 99, 108 Split a levy across units by unit entitlement, reconciled to the cent, with the 3/4-vote and unanimous-vote routes explained. Quorum calculator SPA s. 48 How many votes you need present in person or by proxy — plus the adjournment rule when you fall short. Voting threshold checker SPA ss. 1, 71, 96, 108, 128 Nineteen decisions mapped to the vote each one needs — majority, 3/4, 80% or unanimous — with the section for every row. Records request deadline SPA ss. 36, 59 Two weeks for records, one week for bylaws or a Form B. Enter the date the request arrived and get the day you must comply by. Why these carry citations

A compliance answer without its source is just an opinion. Every figure in these tools was read from the official consolidation at bclaws.gov.bc.ca rather than from memory or a summary site, and each page shows the section and the date it was checked. Where the law is genuinely unsettled — the 20-versus-21-day notice count, for instance — the tool says so instead of picking a side quietly.

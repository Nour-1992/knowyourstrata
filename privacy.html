Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not.

Short version: the calculators run entirely in your browser and never send what you type. The only personal information this site ever receives is an email address you choose to give it, and the details Stripe needs if you buy something. Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

This policy covers knowyourstrata.com, operated by Nour Attaallah from Connecticut, United States. Questions about anything on this page go to hello@knowyourstrata.com.

The calculators do not send what you type

Every tool on this site — notice deadlines, quorum, special levies, voting thresholds, records deadlines, reserve fund and depreciation report dates, the minutes generator — runs as JavaScript inside your own browser. The meeting dates, unit counts, unit entitlements, budget figures and text you enter are used to calculate an answer on your device and are never transmitted to this site or to anyone else.

Two consequences worth knowing. Nothing you enter is stored anywhere I can see, so I cannot recover it for you. And closing or reloading the page clears it.

Inside the paid Board Starter Pack, checklist ticks are saved in your browser's local storage and template drafts in session storage, both purely so your progress survives a page reload. That data stays in your browser, is never transmitted, and is cleared when you clear your browser data.

The email list

What is collected: your email address, and nothing else. There is no name field, no organisation field, no strata number.

Why: to send you the compliance cheat sheet and to tell you when legislation covered by these tools changes.

Who processes it: the list is hosted by beehiiv, in the United States. Your address is sent to beehiiv along with a tag recording that you subscribed from this website, so I can tell which pages bring subscribers. beehiiv records ordinary email delivery events such as opens and clicks.

Unsubscribing: every email carries an unsubscribe link, and it works immediately. You can also write to hello@knowyourstrata.com and ask to be removed, and I will do it and confirm.

What never happens: your address is never sold, rented, traded, or shared with any advertiser, sponsor, manager, lawyer, insurer or other third party. It is used for the list and nothing else.

If you buy a Board Starter Pack

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout, never on this site, and this site never sees or stores them. Stripe collects what it needs to take a payment and meet its own legal obligations, including your email address, billing country and card information, and processes it under Stripe's privacy policy.

What reaches this site after a payment is limited: confirmation from Stripe that a particular checkout session was paid, which of the two packs it was for, and your email address so the access link can be sent to you. The access link email is sent through Resend. Purchase records are retained for accounting and refund purposes.

Cookies

This site sets exactly one cookie, and only if you buy something.

kys_access
Set after a successful purchase. It holds a signed token that records which pack was paid for, so the pack page opens for you and not for everyone. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, scoped to the pack page path, and contains no name, no email address and no tracking identifier. It is strictly necessary to deliver the thing you paid for. It is long-lived on purpose, so the pack keeps opening years later.

There are no advertising cookies, no social media cookies, no cross-site trackers and no fingerprinting scripts anywhere on this site. That is also why you are not being asked to dismiss a consent banner.

Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first by design: it uses no cookies, does not fingerprint visitors, does not track people across sites, and reports only aggregate figures such as how many times a page was viewed. I use it to see which tools are actually used. It cannot tell me who you are.

Counting how the site is used

Alongside that, the site keeps its own small count of five things: a calculator being run (and which one), a Starter Pack page being opened, the checkout link being clicked, an address joining the list, and a pack being bought. I use it to see which tools are worth building more of, and whether anyone who opens the pack page actually buys it.

It is worth being precise about what that does and does not involve, because it would be easy to write "we use analytics" and leave you guessing:

  • It is first party. The counts go to this site, not to any analytics company. Nothing about you leaves knowyourstrata.com.
  • It stores a running total per day, and nothing else — no visitor identifier, no session, no cookie, no IP address, no user agent, no referrer. There is no column in the table for any of those, so none can be added later by accident.
  • Because of that, it cannot tell one person from another. Two people running the same calculator and one person running it twice are the same number. That is a real accuracy cost, and it is the price of the paragraph above.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is counted at all. Nothing identifying would have been sent either way; honouring the signal is the point.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data — IP address, user agent, the URL requested, timestamps — in order to serve pages, block attacks and keep the site up. That processing is Cloudflare's, under its own terms, and is not used by me to build any profile of visitors.

Where information is held

This site is operated from the United States, and beehiiv, Stripe, Resend and Cloudflare are all United States companies. If you are in Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia or the European Economic Area, information you give this site is processed in the United States and may be accessible to United States authorities under United States law. Subscribing or purchasing is your consent to that transfer.

Your choices and your rights

Whatever framework applies where you live, this is what I will do on request, at no charge:

  • Tell you what I hold about you. In practice that is an email address on the list, and a purchase record if you bought a pack.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete it. The list entry goes immediately. Purchase records have to be kept for a period for accounting and tax reasons, and deleting one ends your access to the pack.
  • Stop emailing you. Unsubscribe link, or ask.

Write to hello@knowyourstrata.com. If you are in Canada and are not satisfied with how a request was handled, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

This site is built for condominium and strata boards and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who processes it, the date below changes and the change is described in the next email to the list. There is no version of this policy that permits selling your information; that is not a promise I intend to revisit.

What to write about

What I cannot answer

I am not a lawyer, and nothing sent here or replied to creates a lawyer-client relationship. I cannot tell you what to do about your specific dispute, interpret your registered bylaws, review your minutes, or advise on a matter that is before a tribunal or headed there.

For that kind of help, go to a strata or condominium lawyer in your province. These are also the right places to start, and they are free:

  • British Columbia — the Condominium Home Owners Association of B.C. (CHOA) and the Vancouver Island Strata Owners Association (VISOA) both run advice lines for members. The Civil Resolution Tribunal handles most strata disputes.
  • Ontario — the Condominium Authority of Ontario (CAO) publishes guidance and runs the Condominium Authority Tribunal. The Canadian Condominium Institute has chapters across the province.

How long a reply takes

Realistically, a few days. Corrections and purchase problems are answered first. If a message has not been answered in a week, it did not arrive, so send it again.

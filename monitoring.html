Legislative monitoring

What we check every Monday, and what has actually changed.

Every tool on this site is built from the official consolidations, and every one of them carries the date a person last read the sections it relies on. This page is the other half of that promise: the thirteen sources an automated watcher re-reads every Monday, and the complete public record of what it has found since it started.

Since 23 August 2026, no legal text has changed in either province.

Every change detected in that window has been a consolidation stamp advancing while the text in force stayed identical. The operative text of the Strata Property Act, the Strata Property Regulation, the Schedule of Standard Bylaws, the Interpretation Act, the Condominium Act, 1998 and O. Reg. 48/01 has not moved once. The deadlines and thresholds a board relied on when monitoring began are the ones in force today, and the record below lists every check.

Why this page exists

A compliance answer is only as good as the day it was last checked. Most published guidance on strata and condominium law does not say when it was checked, which means a reader cannot tell a page written last month from one written in 2015 and never revisited. Several of the deadline explainers still circulating in British Columbia predate Regulation changes that have since taken effect.

So rather than assert that this site is current, here is the evidence. The watcher is a scheduled job that fetches each source directly from the government publisher, strips the consolidation stamp out of the compared text, and reports whether the remaining legal text differs from the copy it held the week before. It detects and alerts. It never edits a page on this site. A detected change in legal text triggers a human re-reading before anything here is updated, and that reading is what moves a page's verified date.

The two kinds of change, and why the difference matters

Not every detected change means the law moved, and conflating the two is how a site ends up either crying wolf or missing a real amendment.

A consolidation stamp advanced
The publisher reissued the consolidation with a newer currency date while the text in force stayed identical. This is a bookkeeping event. It updates the date printed in a tool footer and nothing else. Every change recorded below so far has been of this kind.
The legal text changed
A section was amended, added or repealed, or a new consolidation version appeared in Ontario's version list. This is the event the watcher exists to catch. It stops the automated path entirely and hands the section to a person to read against the source before any tool is touched. This has not happened since monitoring began.

What this page does not claim

  • It says nothing about the period before 23 August 2026. The watcher cannot prove a source was unchanged over a week it was not watching, so it does not try. Where a tool on this site was last read by a person before that date, its footer still shows the older consolidation it was read against, rather than borrowing this page's evidence.
  • A quiet week is not a legal opinion. An unchanged consolidation means the published text did not move. It does not mean an amendment has not been passed and is waiting to be proclaimed, and it does not mean a court has not read an unchanged section in a new way.
  • It does not cover your bylaws. Your corporation's registered bylaws can add requirements or vary what applies. Nothing automated can see them.
  • It is not legal advice. For anything contested, talk to a strata or condominium lawyer in your province.

Use this

If you publish guidance on British Columbia or Ontario condominium law and you want to know whether the sections you cite have moved, you are welcome to rely on this page and to link to it. If you would rather be told than have to check, the weekly note goes out to a small list and says the same thing this page says, in a paragraph.

If you find an error here, or a change the watcher missed, that takes priority over everything else on the list. Write to hello@knowyourstrata.com.

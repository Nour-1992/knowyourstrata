What an answer looks like here

Send notice by mail or email no later than Friday, February 26, 2027

14 days of notice, plus 2 for the clear-day counting rule, plus 4 because notice sent by mail or email is deemed received four days later. Councils that count 14 days on a calendar send notice late.

Strata Property Act ss. 45, 61 · Interpretation Act ss. 25, 25.2

Why you can forward these to your board

Compliance answers are only worth anything if you can show where they came from. Nothing is published until its section has been read from the official consolidation: bclaws.gov.bc.ca for British Columbia and ontario.ca e-Laws for Ontario.

  1. ReadEach tool is built from the official consolidation, never from memory or a summary site.
  2. CalculateThe counting rules are applied in the open: clear days, deemed receipt, holidays, unit entitlement.
  3. Re-checkAn automated watcher re-reads the sources every Monday. A change triggers a human re-read before anything moves.
  • Every figure carries the exact subsection it rests on, down to s. 108(2)(b) rather than a vague "section 108".
  • Every page shows the date its law was last checked, and the consolidation that text was current to.
  • Where the law is genuinely unsettled, the tool says so — the 20-versus-21-day notice count is shown as a real disagreement, not resolved quietly.
  • Where your own bylaws can override the Act, the answer tells you which rule is which.
  • The official consolidations behind these tools are re-read every Monday by an automated watcher, and every check is published, including the weeks nothing changed.

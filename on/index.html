Ontario · Condominium Act, 1998

Ontario condo compliance tools

Tools for Ontario condominium boards, built on the Condominium Act, 1998. Ontario is newer here than British Columbia, so the list is short on purpose — nothing gets published until its sections have been read against the official statute.

✓ Every live tool cited & dated

Quorum calculator Condo Act s. 50 25% of the units — and the reduced 15% that applies only on a third attempt at an AGM or turn-over meeting, not to a requisitioned one. Notice of meeting deadline Condo Act s. 47 Preliminary Notice and Notice of Meeting send-by dates, board-called or owner-requisitioned. Requisition threshold checker Condo Act s. 46 What share of units is needed to requisition a meeting, the current 35/45-day timeline, and what changes December 31, 2026. Records request deadline Condo Act s. 55 Board's-response and delivery deadlines for an owner's request for records — core and non-core records run different clocks. Reserve fund study deadline Condo Act s. 94 When the next study is due on a rolling 3-year clock, and which of the three study classes it must be. Status certificate deadline Condo Act s. 76 The day a certificate is due, the $100 tax-inclusive fee cap, and the clean certificate the Act deems issued if the board is late. Voting threshold checker Condo Act ss. 56, 97, 107 The vote a by-law, a rule, a common element change or a declaration amendment actually needs, including the prescribed by-laws that need only a majority of the room. Insurance deductible chargeback Condo Act s. 105 Whether a deductible can be charged back to an owner, how much, and the own-unit limit in s. 105(2) that decides most of these wrong. Common expense calculator Condo Act s. 84 Splits the annual budget across units by the proportions in your declaration, to the cent, and checks the s. 93(5) reserve fund floor. Lien deadline calculator Condo Act s. 85 The lien expires three months after the default. Works out that date, the 10 days notice the owner is owed first, and the encumbrancer notice that keeps the lien in priority. AGM deadline Condo Act s. 45(2) The AGM is due within six months of the fiscal year end, counted the Legislation Act way. Gets that date and the latest preliminary notice, notice of meeting and record date. Information certificate deadline O. Reg. 48/01 s. 11.1 The two periodic information certificate dates in your fiscal year, 60 days after the first and third quarters, and the 30 and 5 day update deadlines. Ontario law is mid-transition — so the date matters

The Condominium Act, 1998 has been amended in stages, with provisions coming into force at different times. That makes a verified date more than housekeeping here: it tells you which text was actually read. Section 50 was read from the official statute on ontario.ca, and the page says so alongside the answer.

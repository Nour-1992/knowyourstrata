Ontario · Condominium Act, 1998

Ontario condo compliance tools

Tools for Ontario condominium boards, built on the Condominium Act, 1998. Ontario is newer here than British Columbia, so the list is short on purpose — nothing gets published until its sections have been read against the official statute.

✓ Every live tool cited & dated

Quorum calculator Condo Act s. 50 25% of the units — and the reduced 15% that applies only on a third attempt at an AGM or turn-over meeting, not to a requisitioned one. Notice of meeting deadline Condo Act s. 47 Preliminary Notice and Notice of Meeting send-by dates, board-called or owner-requisitioned. Requisition threshold checker Condo Act s. 46 What share of units is needed to requisition a meeting, the current 35/45-day timeline, and what changes December 31, 2026. Records request deadline Condo Act s. 55 Board's-response and delivery deadlines for an owner's request for records — core and non-core records run different clocks. Reserve fund study deadline Condo Act s. 94 When the next study is due on a rolling 3-year clock, and which of the three study classes it must be.

Ontario law is mid-transition — so the date matters

The Condominium Act, 1998 has been amended in stages, with provisions coming into force at different times. That makes a verified date more than housekeeping here: it tells you which text was actually read. Section 50 was read from the official statute on ontario.ca, and the page says so alongside the answer.
